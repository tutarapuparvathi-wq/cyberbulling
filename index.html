📚 Awareness

Know the signs of cyberbullying

Cyberbullying can happen through social media, chats, games, comments, and other digital spaces.

💬

Repeated insults

Persistent name-calling, humiliation, threats, or abusive comments can be warning signs.

📸

Sharing without consent

Posting private photos, messages, or personal information to embarrass someone can cause harm.

🚫

Deliberate exclusion

Repeatedly excluding or targeting someone online may be part of harmful behavior.

🧭 What to do

Pause. Protect. Report.

If a message feels threatening or abusive, avoid escalating the conversation. Save evidence, block the account when appropriate, and use the platform’s reporting tools.
